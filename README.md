# Halli-Santhe Digital – Hyperlocal Artisan Marketplace Platform

## Overview
Halli-Santhe Digital is an Android-based hyperlocal marketplace application developed to help local artisans and rural sellers digitally showcase and promote their products beyond traditional weekly markets. The platform enables direct product visibility and customer interaction through a simple digital catalog system.

## Features
- Artisan product upload system
- Hyperlocal product catalog
- Category-wise product browsing
- Product availability inquiry
- Seller communication support
- Traditional craft promotion
- Mobile-friendly marketplace interface

## Technologies Used
- Android Studio
- Java/Kotlin
- Firebase Firestore
- RecyclerView & GridLayoutManager
- Room Database
- Image Compression Techniques

## Problem Statement
Many local artisans and village sellers depend only on physical weekly markets, limiting their reach and reducing visibility to potential customers.

## App Workflow
- Upload product details and images
- Browse products by category
- View artisan offerings digitally
- Contact sellers for product inquiries
- Explore traditional and local crafts

## Technical Implementation
- RecyclerView with GridLayoutManager for catalog display
- Firebase Firestore for product management
- Image compression for optimized storage
- Responsive UI for mobile marketplace accessibility

## Impact Goals
- Promote local artisan businesses
- Preserve traditional crafts and products
- Support rural digital commerce
- Improve direct customer accessibility
